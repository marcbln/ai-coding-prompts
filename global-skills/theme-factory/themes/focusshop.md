# Focusshop

A clean and approachable e-commerce theme built around a single bold primary accent on a light, minimal canvas. Inspired by the Focusshop brand guidelines.

## Color Palette

- **Focusshop Red**: `#e03e2d` - Primary brand accent (CTAs, links, highlights)
- **Soft Coral**: `#e96354` - Lighter accent for hovers and secondary emphasis
- **Blush**: `#f2d0c8` - Soft tint for tags, badges and subtle fills
- **Off White**: `#f6f7f7` - Light background and panel fills
- **Mist Gray**: `#e2e7e9` - Borders, dividers and hairline rules
- **Warm Gray**: `#9da19f` - Secondary / muted text
- **Charcoal**: `#2b2b2b` - Body text and dark elements

## Typography

- **Headers**: Roboto Bold
- **Body Text**: Roboto

## Best Used For

E-commerce product pages, online shops, retail and catalog content, web stores and shopping-related presentations.
